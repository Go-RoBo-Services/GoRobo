# 🤖 Go RoBo

### Robotics & DIY Electronics Catalog

**Go RoBo** is a robotics and DIY electronics component platform designed to make it easy for students, makers, hobbyists, engineers, and robotics enthusiasts to discover and source electronic components, modules, motors, sensors, development boards, and robotic kits.

🌐 **Website:** https://gorobo.amazecc.com

---

## ✨ Features

* 🔎 **Component Search** — Quickly find components by name.
* 🧩 **Category-Based Browsing** — Explore components across multiple electronics and robotics categories.
* 🛒 **Shopping Cart** — Add required components to your cart before ordering.
* 🔧 **Robotics Components** — Motors, actuators, sensors, motor drivers, robotic kits, and more.
* ⚡ **Development Boards** — Arduino, ESP, STM32 and related boards.
* 📡 **Sensors & Modules** — A large collection of sensors and electronic modules.
* 🔌 **Power & Electronics** — Power supplies, converters, resistors, capacitors, transistors, ICs, relays, and accessories.
* 📦 **Component Inquiries** — Browse, filter, and inquire about individual parts.
* 💬 **Direct Support** — Contact the Go RoBo team through WhatsApp or email.
* 🧑‍💻 **Open Source** — Dedicated section for open-source initiatives and projects.
